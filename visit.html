Box office

Opens one hour before every Portal Players production.

We accept cash, debit, Mastercard and Visa.

Parking

Street parking is available around the theatre on Argyle Street, 4th Avenue and nearby streets.

Accessibility

A ramp leads to the main lobby doors on Argyle Street. Need a hand on arrival? Call 250-723-1195 ahead of the show.

Accessible seating and an accessible washroom are available.

Visiting shows

For touring acts and rentals, tickets and door times are handled by each presenter. Check the show page for details.

During the show

No photos or video during performances, please.

Late seating is discouraged, but may be accommodated on occasion.

Contact us

Call 250-723-1195 or email capitoltheatre@shaw.ca. For rentals, use our booking enquiry form.
