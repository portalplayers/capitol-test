Membership

Members make every show happen. Join to act, crew, volunteer and have your say in how the society is run.

Individual

$10

per year

Family

Join online

All memberships expire September 30 each year.

Why join

  • Get on stage or backstage. Only members can take part in productions and activities, including actors, crew, technical operators and volunteers.
  • Have your say. Members in good standing can vote at the Annual General Meeting.
  • Lead the society. Members can run for the board of directors or executive committee.
  • Hear it first. Auditions, workshops and calls for volunteers.
To vote at the AGM, or to run for the board or executive committee, you must have paid your dues and been a member in good standing for 30 days before the meeting.
