The Helen Ford Lobby

Helen Ford

A generous Alberni Valley resident whose bequest renewed the front of The Capitol Theatre.

A gift to the Capitol

Helen Ford's bequest funded a major renovation of The Capitol Theatre. The renewed front of house was named the Helen Ford Lobby in her honour. Her gift also helped Portal Players secure grants from Cultural Spaces Canada, the Province of British Columbia and the Vancouver Foundation.

Her life

Helen first saw the Alberni Valley in 1926, when her father bought the Sproat Lake Logging Co. During the Second World War she served as an officer in the Canadian Women's Army Corps, and she was awarded the M.B.E. in 1946. She married Armour Ford in 1953 and the couple settled at Sproat Lake.

Helen gave her time to the West Coast General Hospital auxiliary, the Community Arts Council and the School District 70 board, and helped found the Alberni District Historical Society in 1965. In 1983 the Fords were named Citizens of the Year.

She later gave 130 acres at Sproat Lake, including a waterfall, to the people of British Columbia as a day-use park. Helen Ford died in May 1997 at age 91, leaving money, documents, books and artifacts to the Alberni District Historical Society. Helen Lake on Porter Ridge is named in her memory.
