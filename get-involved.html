Volunteer

No experience needed. Pick something you love or something you'd like to learn:

  • Acting
  • Directing
  • Assistant directing
  • Show production
  • Costumes
  • Sewing
  • Wardrobe maintenance
  • Sound
  • Lights
  • Set design
  • Set construction
  • Props
  • Make-up
  • Cast party
  • Concession & refreshments
  • Front of house & ushering
  • Theatre maintenance
  • Clean up
  • Publicity
  • Photography
  • Poster design & distribution
  • Box office sales
  • Corporate sponsorships
  • Play writing
  • Play reading
  • Orchestra
  • Chorus
  • Board of directors
  • Zone festival

…and much more!

Volunteers take part as members of Portal Players. Join from $10 a year, then email capitoltheatre@shaw.ca to tell us what interests you.

Auditions & show proposals

Auditions: subscribe to our email newsletter to get notice of upcoming auditions.

Proposing a show: each season is planned about a year ahead. When the season is being developed, our Artistic Direction Committee (ADC) puts out a call for proposals. Subscribe to the email newsletter for updates.

Sponsor Our Season

Partner with Portal Players and turn our promotion into your promotion. Six partnership packages, from $120 for individuals to $3,000+ for businesses, with season tickets, logos, programme ads and more.

See partnership packages

Donate

Portal Players Dramatic Society is a federally registered charitable organization and a provincially registered non-profit society. Your gift keeps a 90-year-old building alive and the stage lit for the next generation, and cash donations are tax deductible. Official donation receipts are issued for income tax purposes.
