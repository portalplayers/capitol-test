Volunteer

Now recruiting

Join the Pen Pals production team

Portal Players and STAGES are looking for volunteers to fill four roles. It’s a great way to gain hands-on theatre experience with a supportive team.

  • Stage manager: keep the show on track, coordinate rehearsals and performances, manage cues and communication
  • Assistant stage manager: support the stage manager, take notes and track cues
  • Lighting designer: create the look of the show, design the lighting plot, choose fixtures and cues
  • Lighting operator: run the console and follow the cue sheet at rehearsals and performances

You must be available for tech, dress, preview and all performances: November 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 20, 21, 27 and 28.

Interested? Contact director Brent Ronning at 250-730-2700 or brent.ronning@shaw.ca, or producer Liane Fitzpatrick at fitz@shaw.ca.

No experience needed. Pick something you love or something you’d like to learn:
