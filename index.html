4904 Argyle Street · Port Alberni

Live on Argyle Street since 1936.

This historic former movie theatre is now home of the performing arts, owned and operated by Portal Players Dramatic Society. Our team of volunteers produce and perform plays and musicals at The Capitol Theatre. When we're not on stage with our own productions, doors are open to touring acts, concerts and local events.

Our story

90 years of curtain calls

  1. 1936The Capitol opens on May 16 with Bette Davis in Dangerous, 500 seats, air conditioning and a chesterfield in the foyer.
  2. 1978Twenty locals meet in the North Island College gym and found Portal Players.
  3. 1989The Players buy the Capitol. Eight months and $150,000 in grants, donations and volunteer sweat later, it reopens with Something's Afoot.
