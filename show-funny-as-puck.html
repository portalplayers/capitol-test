About the show

Former NHL Calgary Flames athletic trainer turned stand-up comedian Brian Patafie wraps up his “Funny as Puck Cross Canada Tour, eh” right here at The Capitol Theatre. The tour opens in Charlottetown, PEI in April and ends in Port Alberni on Saturday, July 24.

Brian has picked some of North America’s funniest stand-up comedians to join him on the road. This show features four headliners in a 90-minute set built for laughs from start to finish.

Known as “Comedy’s Original Enter-Trainer,” Brian has been playing to comedy fans across North America and the United Kingdom since May 2021, and he guarantees his 2027 show will make you laugh your ICE OFF! Expect stories of a dysfunctional upbringing in a military home, becoming a new dad well into his forties, and hockey tales blended into an outrageous stand-up routine.

Advance tickets are on Ticketpro: $38, or $27 for seniors and military, taxes and service fees included. Tickets will also be sold at the door on the night of the show. More about Brian at brianpatafie.com.

This is a visiting show booked into The Capitol Theatre. Tickets, pricing and refunds are handled by the presenter. Every ticket helps Portal Players keep this historic 1936 theatre open.
