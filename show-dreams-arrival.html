About the show

Dreams brings the songs of Fleetwood Mac and Arrival brings the hits of ABBA, back to back on one stage. A night of nostalgia, big harmonies and singalongs.

The show runs about two hours. All sales are final, with no refunds.

This is a visiting show booked into The Capitol Theatre. Tickets, pricing and refunds are handled by the presenter. Every rental helps Portal Players keep the building open.
