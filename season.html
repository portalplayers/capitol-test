How the Season Pass works

  • $79 gets you into one performance of each of the three productions, a saving of $11 over three $30 single tickets.
  • Buy now and choose your dates and seats later.
  • Passes are sold online through ThunderTix, and at the box office when it opens.
  • Coming to Pen Pals? Buy the whole season before the show, or at the box office when it opens, and you’re covered for all three productions.
  • Changing dates: We will do our best to accommodate requests to change performance dates, for Season Pass and single ticket purchases alike. All ticket sales are final, with no refunds.

Prefer single tickets?

Single tickets are $30 per person, per production, and every performance starts at 7:30 PM. Single tickets are on sale now. See Tickets for all the ways to buy.

How our season is chosen

Portal Players plans each season about a year ahead. Production proposals are reviewed every February for the following season. Want to direct or pitch a show? Find out how.
