About the show

No script. No rules. All comedy. Three improvisers go head to head in a comedy showdown the poster sums up as “Whose Line Is It Anyway? meets the Thunderdome.”

The Comedy Department performers have been seen with The Second City, Just for Laughs, CBC and Netflix.

This is a visiting show booked into The Capitol Theatre. Tickets, pricing and refunds are handled by the presenter. Every ticket helps Portal Players keep this historic 1936 theatre open.
