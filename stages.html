STAGES Youth Theatre

Acting classes and productions for young people ages 5 and up, at The Capitol Theatre.

September to June

Classes

STAGES is the youth theatre program of Portal Players Dramatic Society. All classes take place at The Capitol Theatre, with three levels from first-timers to a full spring production.

Ages 5–11

Introductory

A great way to learn the basics and get comfortable performing on stage.

Two class times, so you can choose the day that works best: Tuesdays 4–4:55 pm or Thursdays 4–4:55 pm.

  • $645 full year September–June
  • $258 + $387 by semester Sept–Dec, then Jan–June
  • $64.50 per month
Ages 10–15

Intermediate

A step up from Introductory. Students take on more responsibility, with some behind-the-scenes work as well as performing on stage. Students audition with a one-minute monologue to join this class.

Thursdays 5–6 pm

  • $670 full year September–June
  • $268 + $402 by semester Sept–Dec, then Jan–June
  • $67 per month
Ages 14+

Company

A step up from Intermediate. Company performs a production with a full run in the spring, takes on a production role as well as an acting role in that show, and works backstage for all other STAGES shows.

Tuesdays 5–7 pm and Wednesdays 4–6 pm

  • $770 full year September–June
  • $308 + $462 by semester Sept–Dec, then Jan–June
  • $77 per month
Registration for new students opens in September and again in January. It closes at the end of those months, when we cast our shows and begin rehearsals. To register, email stagesyouththeatrecompany@gmail.com.

Meet the director

Gabby Eyford is the Director of the STAGES program. She took over as Director in 2024, but has been part of the program for many years. Gabby grew up as a STAGES student, from elementary school all the way through high school. She then studied at VanArts in Vancouver, graduating from the Acting for Film and Television program in 2020. Shortly after, she returned to Port Alberni and began assisting with STAGES.

Gabby has been overjoyed to see the program flourish over the past few years. She loves creating a welcoming and safe space for students to express themselves.
