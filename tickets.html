Tickets

Our own productions are sold by Portal Players. Visiting shows are sold by each presenter. Here is where to buy every show.

Portal Players Presents

Season Pass $79, or $30 per person per show

The pass covers one performance of each of our three productions; choose dates and seats later. Single tickets are $30 per person, per production. All performances start at 7:30 PM.

At the door: the box office opens one hour before every Portal Players production and takes cash, debit, Mastercard and Visa. Season Passes are also available there. For visiting shows, check the presenter’s ticket site for door times.
Portal Players productions: We will do our best to accommodate requests to change performance dates, for Season Pass and single ticket purchases alike. All ticket sales are final, with no refunds.
